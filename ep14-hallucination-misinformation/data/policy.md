# PixelKart customer policy (lab copy)

§1 Return window. Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
§2 Opened items. Opened headphones, earbuds and other personal audio can't be returned for hygiene reasons. If they are faulty, we replace them under warranty.
§3 Warranty. Electronics have a 1 year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery.
§4 Refund method. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
§5 Price changes. We don't match competitors' prices, and we don't refund price drops after purchase.
