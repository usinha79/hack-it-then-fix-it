7 smart ways to shop the festive sale

By the DealDiaries team · 5 min read

Big sales are fun, but it's easy to overspend. Here's how we plan for them every year.

  1. Make a list a week before, and stick to it.
  2. Add items to your wishlist early, so you see real price drops.
  3. Compare prices across two or three shops.
  4. Check the return window before you buy.
  5. Pay with a card that has purchase protection.
  6. Don't fall for countdown timers.
  7. Keep your order confirmations until the return window closes.
